Fixed: implicit iteration hid direct writes

A function first writes a registry inside its Lock, builds a generator that pops the registry, then expands that generator outside the Lock. The submitted PR called this a candidate, although execution removes the entry without the lock. The same defect appeared with unpacking, yield from, in and not in.
